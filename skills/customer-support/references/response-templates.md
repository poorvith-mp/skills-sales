# Customer Support Response Templates

Standard response templates for tier-1 support scenarios.

## Billing & Invoices
> Hi [Name],
> 
> Thanks for reaching out about your invoice. I reviewed your account and confirmed the duplicate charge from [Date].
> 
> I've initiated a full refund of $[Amount] to your original payment method. You should see that reflected in 3–5 business days. Your next billing date remains [Date].
> 
> Let me know if you need an updated PDF receipt or have any other questions.

## Service Outages & Degraded Performance
> Hi [Name],
> 
> Thank you for reporting this. Our engineering team identified an incident affecting [Feature/Service] starting at [Time].
> 
> A fix is currently deploying, and services are returning to normal. You can track real-time incident status at [Status URL].
> 
> I will follow up here as soon as our post-incident checks are complete.
